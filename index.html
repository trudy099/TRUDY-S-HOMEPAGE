Discover Our School

Introduction

We humbly welcome all new students to this great and mighty school, Uganda Martyrs Secondary School Namugongo, located in Kampala, Uganda.

Our school is Catholic-founded, and we put God first in all activities that take place within the school community. We promote academic excellence, discipline, good morals and the development of every student's talents.

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To be a leading school nurturing lifelong learners who inspire positive change worldwide, promote excellence and foster integrity.

Our Mission

To empower students with knowledge and character to thrive in a dynamic world.
